# Changelog

What changed in each release, newest first. Each section becomes that release's notes.

## 0.1.0

The first release of Gallery: a local photo and video gallery for Android 16 and up, with no account, no cloud, no internet access and no Google services. It comes in two editions with the same app id: Gallery, and Gallery Plus, which adds on-device recognition for search. Either installs over the other.

### Browsing
- Photos grid of every photo and video, newest first, under Today, Yesterday, weekday and date headers; pinch between large, normal and month views, with a fast-scroll handle showing the month.
- Videos tab with every video.
- Collections: Favorites, Trash, and every folder as an album, camera and screenshots first. Make albums, and move or copy photos into them.
- On this day cards with photos from past years around today's date, played as a slideshow.
- Dates come from the photo itself, then its file name, then the file's change time; photos copied or moved out of the Locked folder keep their dates.

### Viewing and editing
- Full-screen viewer: swipe, pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag down to close, drag up for details including camera settings. Videos play in place; motion photos play their moment of video.
- Editor: crop (free or fixed shapes), straighten, rotate, flip, brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and filters, with a live preview and hold to compare. Save over the original or as a copy.
- Slideshow, and set as home screen, lock screen or both.

### Organising
- Select by long-press and drag, or a whole day at once; share, favorite, add to an album or trash.
- Duplicates: finds copies and near-identical shots, suggests which to keep, and trashes the rest.
- Locked folder behind your screen lock, hidden from other apps, with screenshots blocked and never backed up.
- Opens photos and videos from other apps and the camera, and lets other apps pick photos from Gallery.

### Search
- Search by file or album name, year, month, or kind ("videos", "favorites", "screenshots"), from a search bar with Settings on its right.

### Gallery Plus
- Reads the text in photos so search finds it, using an 8-bit text reader that's twice as fast as the original model.
- Finds people's faces and groups them, so you can name people and search for them.
- Search by description, like "a black dog" or "old log cabin", worked out on the phone.
- Getting photos ready happens in the background, newest photos first, using the fast processor cores while charging, with a progress notification and a count in Settings → Text and people.
